Big dental decisions deserve time. Here’s exactly what happens at your complimentary consultation.

Questions

Good to know before you book

Still wondering about something? Call us at (408) 555-0123. A real person answers.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your cosmetic or implant consultation, including the 3D scan and written treatment plan, is complimentary for new patients. There’s no obligation to go ahead.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients say it’s easier than they expected. We use local anesthetic and offer sedation options, and guided placement keeps the procedure precise and recovery short. Most people return to work the next day.

How long do implants and veneers last?

With good care, implants can last a lifetime, and porcelain veneers typically last 10–15 years or more. We’ll explain how to keep your results looking their best.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Many of our patients hadn’t been to a dentist in years. We go at your pace, explain everything before we do it, and offer comfort options including sedation.

Do you have evening or Saturday appointments?

Yes. We’re open until 6pm on weekdays and Saturday mornings, so you can fit care around work and family.
