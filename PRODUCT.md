# Silicon Valley Smile Solutions

## What it is
An independent dental practice in San Jose, California, offering general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry. The website's job is to turn a first-time visitor into a booked consultation.

## Primary audience
Adults in the South Bay weighing a cosmetic or restorative treatment: dental implants, Invisalign/clear aligners, veneers, whitening, crowns. They are researching a significant, often elective, investment and are comparing several practices. They want to be sure of the dentist's skill, see real results, understand cost and financing, and feel they will be treated as a person, not a case.

## What they must believe
- The dentists are highly skilled and experienced with complex cosmetic and implant work.
- Results are natural-looking, not "done."
- The practice is warm, local, and honest: clear pricing conversations, no pressure, flexible financing.
- Booking is easy and low-commitment (free/complimentary consultation — CONFIRM with owner).

## Primary action
Book a consultation (online or by phone).

## Tone
Warm and neighborly, confident without hype. Plain language over clinical jargon. First-person plural ("we").

## Facts status (assumptions, replace before launch)
All of the following are placeholders and must be confirmed by the owner: dentist names, credentials and years in practice, street address, phone number, hours, review ratings/counts, patient testimonials, before/after photos, accepted insurance, financing partners, complimentary-consult offer.

## Brand commitments
- Name: Silicon Valley Smile Solutions. No existing logo, colors, or type.

## Design commitment (2026-09-22)
Owner chose the category-standard dental look, executed at top quality: bright, clean, photo-led, warm and neighborly rather than clinical. Benchmarks for craft level: the best boutique US practice sites (e.g. Tend, modern concierge practices).
